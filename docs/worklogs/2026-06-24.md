## B. DAEX acoustic finding (first §7 Test B bench result)
**A surface exciter needs a coupling surface/cavity to radiate — confirmed by ear.** Free-air the
DAEX is muffled and harmony separation collapses (upper partials don't radiate → parts smear).
Bonded to a small rigid cavity (a cylindrical cup) it opens to ~Mac-speaker clarity and the harmony
reads again. The harmony is fully present in the signal (throat-mic → **Mac speaker** is clear, with
harmony) → purely an acoustic-coupling effect, not signal/engine. **This validates the perform-mode
"DAEX on a rigid cup" design**: the cavity *is* the radiator; its material/size/coupling is the
timbre lever. Bass stays weak (small cavity) → upper parts (Alto/Sop) reproduce better than Bass.

## C. Audio front-end / connector debugging (the integration headache)
Goal for mask integration: **one USB card carrying mic-in + line-out** (one cable to mount).
- **Throat mic = Topiky covert mic, CTIA 4-pole** (male). **UGREEN card = separate TRS female jacks**
  (pink mic / green out). CTIA-into-TRS lands the mic on the wrong pin → measured **near-silent
  (peak ≈ 0.0012, ADC noise only)**; built-in mic sanity check read 0.86, so the capture path was fine.
- **Fix = "CTIA female → 2× male (mic+headphone)" splitter** — throat mic → splitter female,
  **red/mic male → UGREEN pink**, green leg unused (future learn-mode earpiece monitor). Ordered
  SUCESO 3.5mm headset splitter. A first cable arrived as the **reverse gender** ("1 male → 2 female"
  combiner) vs its "Female→2 Male" listing → reported not-as-described, re-ordered.
- **PAM8403 feed stays 3-pole TRS into the green out — NOT changed to 4-pole** (4-pole is mic-side only).
- **Interim (splitter shipping):** throat mic on the **Mac built-in TRRS jack** (works) + PAM8403 on
  UGREEN green out → perform-mode / DAEX-cavity testing is not blocked.
