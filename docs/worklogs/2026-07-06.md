## 2. Studio render stability (`cc91deb9`)
Root causes of "render 不穩", found by A/B on real takes (user's ear = judge):
- glide never enabled offline (scrape at every boundary);
- per-frame pitch tracking let the singer's jitter modulate the shift → **score-to-score shift**;
- singer's wobble copied ×4 in parallel → **grid pinning** (slew-limited 2 st/s — an unlimited
  correction CHASES slides and produces audible counter-slides);
- transitions: glide 0.4 st/hop won by ear; shift JUMPS during unvoiced gaps.
Verified: official `render_score` byte-identical to the by-ear-approved variant. FINDINGS updated.
